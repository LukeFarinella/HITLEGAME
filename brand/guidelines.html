01 Positioning

The doctrine behind the mark

§ 01 / 06

GORGON is not a hardware vendor and not a software vendor — it is a perception-to-decision company. The brand has to read as restrained, exact, and a little unsettling: the calm of a system that has already seen you.

V/01

Say less, mean more

Terse, declarative, technical. No adjectives that a spec sheet wouldn't use. Confidence comes from precision, not volume.

V/02

Instruments, not illustrations

Every graphic element should look like it's reporting a real value — coordinates, ranges, states. Decoration that carries no data gets cut.

V/03

Red is a signal

The accent means something: active, targeted, live, alert. If it isn't communicating status, it stays black and grey.

02 Identity

The mark

§ 02 / 06
GORGON

The glyph is a reticle-eye: a targeting crosshair resolving into a pupil. It is the whole brand in one form — perception and intent, fused. The second O of the wordmark is always the eye's red.
